Proptroller wiring guides

Short, safe how-tos for the most common hookups. Each guide is a 3D scene you can turn around, with big plain steps and a voice that reads them out. New here? Start with Meet your board: it shows how to open the board's page on your phone. Everything here is low voltage: these guides never show mains (wall power) wiring.

Every guide also has a one-page written version with a parts list and a "Not working?" list.
