# 01 Light-up ghost: a motion sensor lights a ghost, which fades out again.
#
# Wiring:
#   motion sensor (dry contact or open-collector output)   -> TRIG1 jack (tip and sleeve)
#   12 V LED strip inside the ghost: + to OUT1 +, - to OUT1 -
#   power the board from 12 V (OUT1 gives the board's supply voltage). Fading dims the strip: keep it to
#   2 A or less (1 A on a 24 V supply).
#
#        [motion sensor]--TRIG1        OUT1--[12 V LED strip]
#
# The sensor holds its contact closed while it sees someone: the ghost lights once per visit,
# and the 20 s sleep at the end is a rest before it can light again.

effect ghost:
  wait for trig1
  fade out1 to 100% over 1.5
  sleep 6
  fade out1 to 0% over 3
  sleep 20
